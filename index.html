Ads are a scaling tool. They amplify what's already there—good or bad.

Hi, I'm Sasha. I've spent 7+ years running paid ads across agencies, in-house marketing teams, and for my own clients. I've seen the same mistakes over and over:

  • Running generic strategies that don't fit the brand.
  • Wasting ad spend on creatives that don’t convert.
  • Making ad decisions based on guesses instead of data.

I got tired of watching that happen and built a different approach that connects strategy, creatives, data and your goals. Together we look at the whole picture: what's worth scaling, what needs work and how to turn the attention you have into predictable revenue.

And if I don't think you're ready for ads yet, I'll tell you that upfront.

Here’s how we
approach ads:

01

Full-funnel thinking

Before scaling your ads, I look at your landing page, messaging, positioning, and conversion flow. We fix what's not working before increasing spend.

02

Deep audience empathy

Ads convert when they resonate. We nail your audience's desires and pains, turning them into creatives that feel like “this is exactly for me.”

03

Decisions guided by data

Opinions don't matter, data does. I look at what resonates, double down on what works, and ruthlessly cut what doesn’t.

04

Consistent testing

The brands that win keep testing. I explore new angles, creative directions, and use AI tools to ensure your ads stand out.

05

06
